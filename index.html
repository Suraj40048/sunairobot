The Lineup

Three tiers of solar panel cleaning automation.

Every site is different — a small rooftop, a mid-size ground mount, or a sprawling utility farm. SUNAIROBOT's tiers scale automation to match the site, the budget, and the crew.

01

Lift & Shift Manual-Guided

A lightweight solar panel cleaning unit that a technician lifts and shifts from panel row to row by hand. Built for small rooftops and low-density installations where a full robotic rail isn't justified.

  • Dry PBT material + brush cleaning
  • Lightweight, two-person operation
  • No fixed rail installation needed
  • Lowest entry cost in the lineup
Automation Manual
Best for Rooftop / small arrays

02

Semi-Automatic Rail-Assisted

A motorised solar panel cleaning head that rides along a mounted panel border and bridges across each row, controlled and repositioned by an operator between rows. It balances speed and cost for mid-size ground-mount plants.

  • Motorised brush & rail-guided motion
  • One operator covers multiple rows
  • Faster coverage than manual units
  • Modular bridges and binders kit fits most panel layouts
